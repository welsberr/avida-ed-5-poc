Avida-ED 5 proof of concept

Explore digital evolution

Run a small Avida experiment, observe digital organisms across generations, and compare how mutation changes the variation in a population.

Try the interactive prototype

The app provides a familiar Avida-ED workspace for setting up a population, running the experiment, and inspecting the results. Live graphs show population size, sequence diversity, and the ancestor's share.

Launch the Avida 5 app

The app may reload once on first use while it enables the shared WebAssembly memory needed for background simulation. Use a modern browser with JavaScript, WebAssembly, and service workers enabled.

About this project

This is an early research and teaching prototype based on Avida 5. It is not a classroom-ready replacement for Avida-ED 4, and the sample experiment is an illustration rather than evidence for a general scientific conclusion.
